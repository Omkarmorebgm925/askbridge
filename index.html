Privacy notice

Privacy notice — preview

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This is a demonstration of an online question-and-answer website, not yet a live multi-user community. The site is served as static files from GitHub Pages.

What is stored

When you enter a display name, question or answer in preview mode, it is stored in your own browser using localStorage. This demonstration does not transmit your posts to a shared AskBridge database. Other visitors cannot see those posts. Deleting this site's browser storage removes them from that browser.

Hosting and external services

GitHub Pages delivers site files and may process request information such as IP addresses and request logs under GitHub’s policies. Read GitHub’s privacy statement.

Do not submit sensitive details

Do not enter patient information, identification numbers, passwords, confidential business information or other sensitive personal information. This preview is not designed for that data.

When this changes

Before enabling public accounts or shared posts, we will revise this notice to specify the actual operator, legal contact details, purposes, service providers, retention periods, and rights or grievance channels as applicable.

Questions or concerns

For a technical or content concern you can open an issue in the public repository. Do not include confidential information in a public issue.

Community rules

Community rules and preview terms

Community rules and preview terms

Last updated: 15 November 2026

AskBridge is currently a demonstration. Questions and replies are saved on your device only, not published to other visitors. The provided sample conversations are fictional.

Acceptable participation

  • Write respectful, relevant and original questions and answers.
  • Do not post threats, harassment, hate, defamatory accusations, scams, illegal material or content that violates others' privacy.
  • Do not copy and publish copyrighted content unless you have permission or another lawful basis.
  • Do not share personal, patient-identifiable or confidential business data.
  • Do not impersonate people or misrepresent credentials.

No professional advice

Community answers may be incorrect. The preview is not medical, legal, financial, engineering certification, or other professional advice; verify high-stakes answers with qualified professionals.

Reports and future moderation

To report a technical issue or a concern about this demonstration, open a GitHub issue without disclosing private information. A public multi-user edition will require a dedicated confidential report channel, moderation process, operator contact details, and an appropriately revised policy before launch.

Brand and availability

AskBridge is a working project name, not a representation that trademarks have been cleared. The preview is provided as-is and may change or be withdrawn. Third-party hosting and database terms apply to those services.
